2. Your API key: you provide your own Zen key. It is stored only on your computer (in a local config file) and is sent only to opencode.ai to process requests. Never share it with anyone.

3. Cloud processing: your spoken commands are converted to text on this PC, but the AI replies are generated in the cloud. Anything you say to JARVIS may pass through OpenCode Zen and its model providers. Do not dictate secrets, passwords, or sensitive personal data.

4. Voice fingerprint: your voice print is computed locally and never leaves your computer. It is used only to verify it is really you speaking.

5. Actions on your PC: JARVIS can open apps, change volume, search files, type text, and more. Dangerous actions (typing, locking, sleeping) always ask for your explicit approval first. You are responsible for commands you give.
